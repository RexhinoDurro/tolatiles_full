<p><strong>TL;DR:</strong> Converting an unused garden tub into a walk-in tile shower is a 3-to-7-day project (demo, plumbing, framing, waterproofing, tile) that typically runs $6,000–$12,000+ and opens up real floor space in a dated 1990s master bath. <p>If you're planning to modernize your home, a custom <a href="/jacksonville/services/bathroom-tile-installation">bathroom tile installation</a> is the centerpiece of any master bath remodel. In many older Jacksonville homes, master bathrooms are dominated by a relic of 1990s design: the massive, built-in garden tub. These deep tubs were once marketed as the ultimate luxury; today they're hard to clean, hard to step into, and rarely used. Converting an underused bathtub into a spacious, custom walk-in shower is one of the most popular and value-adding renovations you can take on.</p>

<span data-media-marker="1" style="display:none"></span>

<h2>Why replace your tub with a walk-in tile shower?</h2>
<ul>
    <li><strong>Maximize floor space.</strong> Standard bathtubs and garden tubs take up a large horizontal footprint. Replacing one with a walk-in shower opens up the layout for double vanities, a linen tower, or simply more room to move.</li>
    <li><strong>Create a spa-like experience.</strong> Converting a tub gives you a bigger shower enclosure that can fit a rainfall showerhead, body sprays, a built-in bench, and multiple soap niches.</li>
    <li><strong>Boost resale value.</strong> Buyers looking at high-end master suites read custom, floor-to-ceiling tilework as a sign of a well-maintained, modernized home.</li>
</ul>

<h2>The step-by-step conversion process</h2>

  </thead>
