<h2>Ventilation: the EPA's top recommendation</h2>
<p>The <a href="https://www.epa.gov/mold/what-are-main-ways-control-moisture-your-home">EPA is direct about this</a>: use exhaust fans in bathrooms to remove moisture to the outside, not into the attic, and run the fan or open a window during and after showering. This single habit addresses the root condition mold needs — sustained surface moisture — more effectively than any cleaning product addresses it after the fact.</p>

<h2>If mold keeps coming back despite cleaning</h2>
<p>The EPA notes that recurring mold in a shower usually resolves with more ventilation and more frequent cleaning. But if it keeps returning at the same grout lines or tile edges despite both, that's a signal worth taking seriously — it can indicate a gap in the waterproofing membrane behind the tile, where moisture is reaching the wall cavity itself rather than just sitting on the surface. That's a structural fix, not a cleaning one.</p>

<span data-media-marker="2" style="display:none"></span>

<h2>Waterproofing membranes: the layer that actually matters</h2>
<p>Surface tile and grout are not themselves waterproof — a proper shower relies on a waterproofing membrane installed behind the tile, sealing seams and penetrations before a single tile is set. See our full <a href="/blog/shower-waterproofing-schluter-systems-explained">shower waterproofing guide</a> for how that system works and why it's the difference between a shower that dries out completely between uses and one that's slowly feeding moisture into the wall.</p>

<h2>Grout sealing closes the surface-level gap</h2>
<p>Unsealed cement grout is porous, which means it holds onto moisture right at the surface — precisely where mold needs it. A grout sealer applied after the grout cures, and reapplied roughly annually in a shower, meaningfully reduces that surface water absorption. See our <a href="/blog/grout-types-sanded-unsanded-epoxy-guide">grout types guide</a> for how epoxy grout sidesteps this issue almost entirely if it's an option for your project.</p>
