<p><strong>TL;DR:</strong> Tile installation typically runs $9–$28 per square foot all-in (materials, labor, grout, underlayment), with ceramic on the low end and porcelain, natural stone, or large-format tile pushing toward $20–$50+. A typical 150-square-foot bathroom runs $1,500–$4,500. The single biggest cost swing isn't the tile itself — it's subfloor condition and tile size/pattern.</p>

<p>Getting a real number for a tile project means separating the tile's price from the installation's price, since they don't always move together. Here's how Jacksonville and St. Augustine tile pricing breaks down for 2026, and what actually moves the total.</p>

<h2>Labor: $4–$22 per square foot</h2>
<p>Labor alone typically runs $4 to $12 per square foot for straightforward installs, and can climb toward $22 for large-format tile, intricate patterns, or difficult access. Labor rate depends on tile size, layout complexity, and room shape — a simple grid layout in an open room costs less to install than the same tile set in a herringbone pattern in a small, cut-up bathroom.</p>

<h2>What actually changes the price</h2>
<p>Three factors move a quote more than the tile itself:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Subfloor condition.</strong> An uneven or damaged subfloor needs self-leveling compound or new backer board before tile can go down — see our <a href="/blog/tile-underlayment-subfloor-prep-guide">underlayment and subfloor prep guide</a>. This is invisible in a quote's tile line item but can be a meaningful share of total cost.</li>
  <li><strong>Tile size and pattern.</strong> Large-format tile (see our <a href="/blog/large-format-gauged-porcelain-tile-what-to-ask">large-format porcelain guide</a>) requires a flatter substrate and more careful handling; herringbone, chevron, and mosaic layouts all add labor time versus a standard grid.</li>
  <li><strong>Waterproofing scope.</strong> A shower or curbless entry needs a full waterproofing membrane system, not just tile — see our <a href="/blog/shower-waterproofing-schluter-systems-explained">shower waterproofing guide</a> for what that adds.</li>
</ul>
